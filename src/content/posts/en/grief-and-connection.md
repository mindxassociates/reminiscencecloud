Reading *When Breath Becomes Air* brought me back to a question that often arises after someone loses a person they love: **How do I move forward without them?**

There is no single way to answer that question. Grief changes the shape of daily life, and it can be painful precisely because the relationship mattered. But moving forward does not always mean leaving someone behind.

A relationship can continue in a different form: through the memories we return to, the values they passed on, the stories we tell, and the small traditions we choose to keep.

## Ways connection can remain

- The memories that still make you smile or pause.
- The values and lessons you carry into your own choices.
- The stories you share with family and friends.
- The rituals, foods, places, or music that still feel connected to them.
- The ways their love helped shape who you are.

Healing is not a demand to forget. Sometimes it is the gradual work of finding a way to carry love while also continuing to live your own life.

Death ends a life, but it does not necessarily end the significance of a relationship. Some relationships simply take a different form.
