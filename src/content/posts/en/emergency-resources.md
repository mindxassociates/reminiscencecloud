## If you need immediate help

If you or someone you know is experiencing an emergency or crisis and needs immediate help, **call [911](tel:911) or go to the nearest emergency room**. Do not wait for an email response or a consultation appointment.

## Crisis and support lines

### 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call [988](tel:988), [text 988](sms:988), or use [online chat](https://988lifeline.org/chat/). Free, confidential support is available 24/7 for emotional distress, suicidal thoughts, or concerns about someone else.

### Crisis Text Line

Text **HOME** to [741741](sms:741741) to connect with a trained volunteer crisis counselor, 24/7. [Official website](https://www.crisistextline.org/text-us/).

### Veterans Crisis Line

Call [988](tel:988) and **press 1**, or text [838255](sms:838255). Support for veterans, service members, and their loved ones is available 24/7. [Official website and chat](https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/).

### National Domestic Violence Hotline

Call [800-799-7233](tel:8007997233) or text **START** to [88788](sms:88788). Support for relationship abuse and domestic violence. [Official website and chat](https://www.thehotline.org/).

### RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline

Call [800-656-4673](tel:8006564673) for support after sexual assault or abuse, 24/7. [Official website and chat](https://rainn.org/help-and-healing/hotline/).

### Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline

Call [800-422-4453](tel:8004224453) or text **GO** to [800-422-4453](sms:8004224453) for guidance and support about child abuse, 24/7. [Official website](https://www.childhelphotline.org/).

### The Trevor Project

Call [866-488-7386](tel:8664887386) or text **START** to [678678](sms:678678). Crisis support for LGBTQ+ young people, 24/7. [Official website and chat](https://www.thetrevorproject.org/get-help/).

### Trans Lifeline

Call [877-565-8860](tel:8775658860) for peer support for trans and questioning people. This service has limited hours; check [current availability on the official website](https://translifeline.org/hotline/).

These resources serve people in the United States. Outside the U.S., contact local emergency services. Service details and availability can change; consult each organization's official website. Links and contact details reviewed September 22, 2026.
