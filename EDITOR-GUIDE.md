# Editor guide

1. Sign in to Pages CMS using the invitation provided by the website administrator.
2. Choose **Reminiscence Cloud**.
3. Open **Site settings**, the relevant page section, **Blog posts (English)**, or **中文博客文章**.
4. Edit the fields and choose **Save**. Saving updates the repository and starts publication.

## Articles

Choose **Blog posts (English) → New** for an English article, or **中文博客文章 → New** for a Chinese article. The two sections are separate: English articles appear only under `/blog/`, while Chinese articles appear only under `/zh/blog/`.

Add a short URL slug using lowercase English words and hyphens, then complete the title, summary, date, accessible image description, search description, and article body. Leave **Published** off while drafting. Turn it on and save only when the article is ready for the public. An article does not need to have a translation in the other language.

Images belong in the media picker. Do not upload private records, client information, credentials, or copyrighted images without permission. Contact the administrator if a layout or menu change is needed.
